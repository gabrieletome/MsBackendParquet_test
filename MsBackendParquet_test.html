Replace functions for peaks data (m/z or intensity) are not applied immediately but cached in the Spectra object. Thus, these are independent of the underlying backend. Replacement functions of spectra variables on the other hand will be applied immediately, in the case of MsBackendMzR adding/replacing the values in the internal data.frame with all spectra metadata, in the case of the MsBackendParquet (presumably) an internal cache.

rt <- rtime(sps_mzr)
microbenchmark(
    sps_mzr$rtime <- rt + 10,
    sps_parq$rtime <- rt + 10,
    times = 10L, unit = "s"
)
#> Unit: seconds
#>                       expr         min          lq         mean       median
#>   sps_mzr$rtime <- rt + 10 0.009294013 0.009542768 0.0113277837 0.0096302645
#>  sps_parq$rtime <- rt + 10 0.000531133 0.000539387 0.0007727617 0.0006527835
#>           uq         max neval cld
#>  0.009802061 0.026572227    10  a 
#>  0.000704801 0.002085725    10   b

expect_equal(rtime(sps_mzr), rtime(sps_parq))
